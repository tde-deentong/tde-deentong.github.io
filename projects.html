    Generative augmentation to reduce spurious correlations

    Research project, University of Melbourne, July to November 2025

    Problem
    Image classifiers often learn the background instead of the object, so they fail on the groups where the two don't usually appear together.
    What I did
    Built a generative augmentation pipeline with SAM2, GroundingDINO and Stable Diffusion that isolates the causal features of each image and reduces background bias. Ran the experiments on HPC clusters with Slurm.
    Result
    Worst-group accuracy rose from 58.9% to 72.8% on ResNet-152, and from 66.67% to 73.33% on ViT-B/32.

    Deep learning, vision-language models, CNNs, transformers

    Climate science fact-checking pipeline

    NLP group project (COMP90042), University of Melbourne, April to May 2025

    Problem
    Checking a climate claim means finding the right evidence among thousands of passages, then deciding whether it supports or refutes the claim.
    What I did
    Designed a hybrid retrieval system combining TF-IDF, MiniLM embeddings and cross-encoder reranking. Fine-tuned RoBERTa for stance classification and fixed its habit of overpredicting the majority class.
    Result
    Retrieval F1 improved by 0.142 over the baseline, and stance macro F1 rose from 0.40 to 0.57.

    NLP, transformers, information retrievalCode on GitHub

    Stability prediction for autonomous agents

    Computer vision project, October to November 2024

    Problem
    A robot stacking objects needs to predict whether a structure will stay standing before it lets go.
    What I did
    Trained ResNet and InceptionV3 models on 7,680 images from the ShapeStack dataset. Experimented systematically with architecture choices such as batch normalisation, learning rates, class weighting and data augmentation.
    Result
    InceptionV3 F1 rose from 0.27 to 0.56 with augmentation, beating the transfer-learning model.

    Computer vision, deep learning
